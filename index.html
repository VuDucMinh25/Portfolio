About Me

I’m a game developer with experience in Unity and C#, I like crafting immersive, interactive experiences. I also have working knowledge of digital art and foundational marketing techniques, which helps me think more creatively. In my free time, I enjoy athletic activities—like jogging and breaking—which inspires my creativity, and sense of momentum in every project, also helps with dealing with stress. Outside of code and pixels, I sometimes do character sketching ideas and experimenting with new gameplay mechanics.

To me, many kids today are less likely to read books and more likely to spend their time playing video games. My goal is to harness that interest by creating games that not only entertain but also help players develop valuable skills and a positive mindset.
